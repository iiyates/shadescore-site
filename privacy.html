The short version

ShadeScore does not collect anything. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no server that belongs to this app. Nothing you do in it is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What the app stores

The app saves your choices on your own device, using the standard iOS preferences store:

  • the stadium, date, start time and event type you last chose
  • your seat: section, row and seat as you typed them, and where on the map you placed it
  • games you have saved, and the names you gave them
  • weather values you typed in by hand

This stays on your device. It is included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone, because that is how iOS backups work. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Location

The app asks for your location only if you tap "Nearest to me" in the stadium list. It asks for your approximate location, uses it once on your phone to sort stadiums by distance, and does not store it or send it anywhere. If you never tap that button, the app never asks. You can turn location off for ShadeScore at any time in Settings.

Network connections

Every stadium, its geometry and its climate data are bundled into the app, so the shade score works with no signal.

  • Satellite view loads map imagery from Apple Maps for the stadium you are looking at. That request goes to Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Official seat map links open the team's or venue's website in your browser. What that site does is covered by its own privacy policy.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.
