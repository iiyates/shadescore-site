ShadeScore Get the app

Last updated 4 November 2026

Privacy

The short version: ShadeScore collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, and no server of its own.

What stays on your phone

  • The stadium, date, time and event type you last chose
  • Your seat: section, row and seat as you typed them, and where you placed it on the map
  • Games you saved, and the names you gave them
  • Weather numbers you typed in yourself

It's included in your iPhone or iPad backup, like any app's settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

Only if you tap "Nearest to me" in the stadium list. The app asks for your approximate location, uses it once on your phone to sort stadiums by distance, and never stores or sends it. You can turn it off any time in Settings.

When the app goes online

  • Forecasts: for games in the next 10 days it asks Apple Weather for the forecast at the stadium. The request holds the stadium's location and the game time, nothing about you.
  • Satellite view loads map imagery from Apple Maps.
  • Official seat map links open the team's or venue's website in your browser.

Apple's services are covered by Apple's privacy policy; websites you open are covered by their own.

Children, changes and contact

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: shadescoreapp@gmail.com
