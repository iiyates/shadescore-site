Shaded seats at NRG Stadium

Where's the shade?

For a football game starting at 1:00 PM on an early-September Sunday, the shadiest seats at NRG Stadium are 25-yard line (SE) (club deck), in shade for about 100% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 36% of that game in shade. NRG Stadium has a retractable roof: with it closed every seat is shaded, and these figures are for the roof open.

Shadiest seats

  • 25-yard line (SE), club deck: 100% of the game in shade
  • 25-yard line (S), club deck: 100% of the game in shade

  • 50-yard line (SW), club deck: 100% of the game in shade
  • Corner (SE), club deck: 90% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (W), club deck: 85% of the game in shade

Sunniest seats

Bring a hat and sunscreen.

  • 50-yard line (W), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (NW), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (N), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 25-yard line (N), lower deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on an early-September Sunday:

  • 1:00 PM start: about 40% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:25 PM start: about 65% of seats' game time in shade
  • 8:20 PM start: about 100% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
