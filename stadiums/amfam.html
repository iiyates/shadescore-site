Where's the shade?

For a baseball game starting at 1:10 PM on a mid-June Saturday, the shadiest seats at American Family Field are right-field foul pole (club deck), in shade for about 85% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 37% of that game in shade. American Family Field has a retractable roof: with it closed every seat is shaded, and these figures are for the roof open.

Sunniest seats

  • Dugout boxes, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Infield, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Left-field foul pole, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on a mid-June Saturday:

  • 1:10 PM start: about 25% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:10 PM start: about 62% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:10 PM start: about 99% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
