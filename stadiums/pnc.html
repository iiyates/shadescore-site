Shaded seats at PNC Park

Where's the shade?

For a baseball game starting at 1:10 PM on a mid-June Saturday, the shadiest seats at PNC Park are right-field foul pole (lower deck), in shade for about 50% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 11% of that game in shade.

Sunniest seats

Bring a hat and sunscreen.

  • Behind home plate, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Dugout boxes, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Infield, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Left-field foul pole, lower deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on a mid-June Saturday:

  • 1:10 PM start: about 2% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:10 PM start: about 41% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:10 PM start: about 96% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
