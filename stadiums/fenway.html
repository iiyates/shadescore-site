Where's the shade?

For a baseball game starting at 1:10 PM on a mid-June Saturday, the shadiest seats at Fenway Park are behind home plate (club deck), in shade for about 100% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 47% of that game in shade.

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on a mid-June Saturday:

  • 1:10 PM start: about 45% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:10 PM start: about 57% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:10 PM start: about 94% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.

About the ShadeScore app

Shade is modelled from Fenway Park's real location, orientation and seating rows. Treat a result near 50% as "the shade line will be close". Weather and roof decisions on the day can change things.
