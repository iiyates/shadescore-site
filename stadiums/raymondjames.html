Where's the shade?

For a football game starting at 1:00 PM on an early-September Sunday, the shadiest seats at Raymond James Stadium are end zone (S) (club deck), in shade for about 98% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 36% of that game in shade.

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on an early-September Sunday:

  • 1:00 PM start: about 38% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:25 PM start: about 66% of seats' game time in shade
  • 8:20 PM start: about 100% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
