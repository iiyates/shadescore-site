Where's the shade?

For a football game starting at 12:00 PM on an early-September Saturday, the shadiest seats at Jordan-Hare Stadium are 50-yard line (SW) (lower deck), in shade for about 43% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 10% of that game in shade.

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on an early-September Saturday:

  • 12:00 PM start: about 1% of seats' game time in shade
  • 3:30 PM start: about 46% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:00 PM start: about 100% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
