Shaded seats at Mountain America Stadium

Where's the shade?

For a football game starting at 12:00 PM on an early-September Saturday, the shadiest seats at Mountain America Stadium are 50-yard line (SW) (lower deck), in shade for about 58% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 17% of that game in shade.

  • 50-yard line (SW), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (W), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (NW), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on an early-September Saturday:

  • 12:00 PM start: about 6% of seats' game time in shade
  • 3:30 PM start: about 51% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:00 PM start: about 100% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
