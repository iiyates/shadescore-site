Where's the shade?

For a baseball game starting at 1:10 PM on a mid-June Saturday, the shadiest seats at Chase Field are behind home plate (club deck), in shade for about 100% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 38% of that game in shade. Chase Field has a retractable roof: with it closed every seat is shaded, and these figures are for the roof open.

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on a mid-June Saturday:

  • 1:10 PM start: about 37% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:10 PM start: about 58% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:10 PM start: about 98% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
