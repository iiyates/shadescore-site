Where's the shade?

For a baseball game starting at 1:10 PM on a mid-June Saturday, the shadiest seats at Target Field are right-field foul pole (club deck), in shade for about 100% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 39% of that game in shade.

Shadiest seats

  • Right-field foul pole, club deck: 100% of the game in shade
  • Infield, first-base side, club deck: 100% of the game in shade
  • Dugout boxes, first-base side, club deck: 100% of the game in shade
  • Behind home plate, club deck: 96% of the game in shade
  • Dugout boxes, third-base side, club deck: 86% of the game in shade
  • Infield, third-base side, club deck: 75% of the game in shade

Sunniest seats

Bring a hat and sunscreen.

  • Behind home plate, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Dugout boxes, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Infield, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Left-field foul pole, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on a mid-June Saturday:

  • 1:10 PM start: about 39% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:10 PM start: about 65% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:10 PM start: about 96% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
