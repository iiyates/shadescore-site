Shaded seats at Rate Field

Where's the shade?

For a baseball game starting at 1:10 PM on a mid-June Saturday, the shadiest seats at Rate Field are behind home plate (club deck), in shade for about 100% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 48% of that game in shade.

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on a mid-June Saturday:

  • 1:10 PM start: about 46% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:10 PM start: about 69% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:10 PM start: about 99% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
