Where's the shade?

For a baseball game starting at 1:10 PM on a mid-June Saturday, the shadiest seats at Wrigley Field are behind home plate (lower deck), in shade for about 33% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 9% of that game in shade.

Shadiest seats

  • Behind home plate, lower deck: 33% of the game in shade
  • Dugout boxes, third-base side, lower deck: 32% of the game in shade
  • Infield, third-base side, lower deck: 30% of the game in shade

Sunniest seats

Bring a hat and sunscreen.

  • Behind home plate, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Dugout boxes, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Infield, third-base side, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • Left-field foul pole, upper deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on a mid-June Saturday:

  • 1:10 PM start: about 3% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:10 PM start: about 38% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:10 PM start: about 91% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.

About the ShadeScore app

Shade is modelled from Wrigley Field's real location, orientation and seating rows. Treat a result near 50% as "the shade line will be close". Weather and roof decisions on the day can change things.
