Where's the shade?

For a football game starting at 12:00 PM on an early-September Saturday, the shadiest seats at Rose Bowl are corner (SW) (lower deck), in shade for about 54% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 12% of that game in shade.

Shadiest seats

  • Corner (S), lower deck: 51% of the game in shade
  • End zone (SE), lower deck: 50% of the game in shade

Sunniest seats

Bring a hat and sunscreen.

  • 50-yard line (N), lower deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (N), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (NE), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (E), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on an early-September Saturday:

  • 12:00 PM start: about 3% of seats' game time in shade
  • 3:30 PM start: about 44% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:00 PM start: about 100% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
