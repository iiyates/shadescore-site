Where's the shade?

For a football game starting at 12:00 PM on an early-September Saturday, the shadiest seats at Memorial Stadium are 50-yard line (S) (lower deck), in shade for about 50% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 12% of that game in shade.

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on an early-September Saturday:

  • 12:00 PM start: about 3% of seats' game time in shade
  • 3:30 PM start: about 36% of seats' game time in shade
  • 7:00 PM start: about 97% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
