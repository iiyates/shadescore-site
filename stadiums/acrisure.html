Where's the shade?

For a football game starting at 1:00 PM on an early-September Sunday, the shadiest seats at Acrisure Stadium are end zone (S) (club deck), in shade for about 100% of the game. Across the whole stadium, seats spend about 40% of that game in shade.

  • Corner (S), club deck: 100% of the game in shade

Sunniest seats

Bring a hat and sunscreen.

  • 50-yard line (N), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (NE), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 50-yard line (E), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade
  • 25-yard line (E), upper deck: 0% of the game in shade

Does the start time matter?

Yes, a lot. Across the stadium on an early-September Sunday:

  • 1:00 PM start: about 43% of seats' game time in shade
  • 4:25 PM start: about 71% of seats' game time in shade
  • 8:20 PM start: about 100% of seats' game time in shade

Night games are in shade once the sun sets.
